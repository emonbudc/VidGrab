Everything in one app

Built for saving,
not scrolling.

From the first copied link to the finished file, VidGrab handles the messy parts. The new home puts Discover, YouTube and Music one tap apart, with smart link detection and downloads that stay out of your way.

Every site, one downloader

YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and 1,800+ more. Paste a link, use the share sheet, or browse in the built-in web view.

Up to 4K, audio included

Pick MP4, WebM, MP3, M4A or Opus. Separate video and audio streams are merged on-device for a clean, playable file.

Whole playlists, one tap

Save an entire playlist or channel, or hand-pick videos. Everything lands in one queue at the quality you chose.

Play anywhere

Background audio, lock-screen controls, picture-in-picture, speed control, subtitles and a real queue — powered by AndroidX Media3.

A browser that finds media

Open a site and VidGrab detects MP4, HLS and embedded players. Keep history, save pages and grab what you find.

Private by default

No account and no ad SDKs. Your library, history, vault and settings live on your phone. Optional privacy toggles are yours.

Gallery and tools

Browse photos by album, trim clips, extract audio, save WhatsApp statuses and keep private files in a PIN vault.

Updates that verify themselves

Signed release APKs, SHA-256 checks and the same signing key across versions. In-app updates download and verify the right build.

The same extractor that powers the app, now on your computer. Search, inspect every format, and save with a resumable download queue.

Chrome / Edge

Browser extension

Detects videos on the page and puts a “Download with VidGrab” button right where you are already looking.

  • Hover overlay on videos and direct media links
  • Popup list of every stream found on the page
  • Right-click “Download with VidGrab” on pages and links
  • Talks to the desktop app over a token-protected loopback bridge
Download extension

Three steps to your first desktop download

  1. 1
    Run the app. Start vidgrab-cli serve and keep the token it prints.
  2. 2
    Load the extension. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, then load the unpacked folder.
  3. 3
    Paste the token. Extension Settings → port 37821 + token. Hover a video and hit Download.

The bridge listens on 127.0.0.1 only and every request needs the token. ffmpeg is required for merging split tracks and HLS. Builds are produced by the Desktop CLI workflow; grab the CLI and extension zips under “Artifacts”.
