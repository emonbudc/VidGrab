Everything in one app

Built for saving,
not scrolling.

From the first copied link to the finished file, VidGrab handles the messy parts. Extract, merge, queue, organise and play — all on your device.

Every site, one downloader

YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and 1,800+ more. Paste a link, use the share sheet, or browse in the built-in web view.

Up to 4K, audio included

Pick MP4, WebM, MP3, M4A or Opus. Separate video and audio streams are merged on-device for a clean, playable file.

Whole playlists, one tap

Save an entire playlist or channel, or hand-pick videos. Everything lands in one queue at the quality you chose.

Play anywhere

Background audio, lock-screen controls, picture-in-picture, speed control, subtitles and a real queue — powered by libVLC.

A browser that finds media

Open a site and VidGrab detects MP4, HLS and embedded players. Keep history, save pages and grab what you find.

Private by default

No account and no ad SDKs. Your library, history, vault and settings live on your phone. Optional privacy toggles are yours.

Gallery and tools

Browse photos by album, trim clips, extract audio, save WhatsApp statuses, clean storage and keep private files in a PIN vault.

Updates that verify themselves

Signed release APKs, SHA-256 checks and the same signing key across versions. In-app updates download and verify the right build.

Always getting better.

Release notes for the builds that shaped VidGrab. Full history lives on GitHub.

v1.0.9 Nov 6, 2026 Latest

Playback rebuilt. Interface reimagined.

  • Playback moved to libVLC with native audio tracks, subtitles and background playback.
  • A new dark, edge-to-edge interface across Home, Play and Settings.
  • Home gets an icon backdrop, text tabs and one-tap link search; downloads get clear progress sections.
  • Fresh logo, launcher icon and splash screen.
  • Signed APKs with SHA-256 checks for arm64, armv7 and universal devices.
